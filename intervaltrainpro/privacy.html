Privacy Policy

IntervalTrainPro for iOS · Last updated: November 25, 2026

In short: IntervalTrainPro does not collect any personal data. There are no user accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no server of my own. Your workout data stays on your device, in Apple Health and – if enabled – in your own iCloud.

2. Data stored in the app

The app stores your settings (e.g. volume, voice, theme), your presets and your workout history locally on your device only. This data is never sent to me. Deleting the app removes it from your device.

3. Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you allow it, the app uses Apple Health:

  • Read: heart rate, to show your current pulse during a workout.
  • Write: completed workouts and active energy (calories), so they appear in your Health app.

Health data is processed on your device only, is never shared with me or any third party and is never used for advertising. The legal basis is your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time under Settings → Apps → Health → Data Access & Devices → IntervalTrainPro.

4. iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled on your device, the app syncs settings, presets and workout history between your devices using the iCloud storage of your own Apple Account. iCloud is provided by Apple; I have no access to this data. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy. You can turn off iCloud for the app in your iOS settings under your Apple Account.

5. Notifications

If you allow it, the app shows local notifications about the next workout phase. They are created on your device; no push server is used.

6. Purchase and App Store

The app is distributed through Apple's App Store. Purchase, payment and download are handled by Apple; I do not receive any data that identifies you personally. Apple may provide developers with aggregated, anonymous statistics if you have agreed to share analytics with developers in your device settings.

7. Contact by email

If you email me, I process your address and message only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete it once it has been handled and no legal retention period applies. My mailbox is provided by Gmail (Google).

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no externally loaded fonts.

9. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and does not collect personal data from children.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw any consent you have given. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since I do not store any data about you through the app, I usually cannot provide information about app data – it stays with you.

11. Changes

If the app changes in a way that involves processing other data, I will update this policy. The version published here applies.
